# zirkle memory.rs is the reload transaction, not just a memory bank

The zirkle core crate fuses two unrelated mechanisms in `memory.rs` (111 lines).

- **Bank half** — `Snapshot { revision, version, bank, state }`, `read()`, `update()` (compare-and-swap). Host-resident cartridge data. Reached from `sdk.rs::memory`/`checkpoint`, `cartridge.rs` `{"memory":"read"|"update"}` RPC, `context.rs` `ctx:memory`/`ctx:checkpoint`, `runtime.rs::Ctx::memory`.
- **Gate half** — `gate` (RwLock), `epoch` (watch channel), `pending` (AtomicBool), `begin`/`finish`/`fork`/`thaw`. This is the in-place cartridge-replacement transaction, not storage. Reached from `loader.rs::replace_entry` (~120 lines) and `request_reload`, the whole of `service.rs`, `lua.rs::to_lua` (the "service is being replaced" guard), `runtime.rs::on` and `Ctx::provide` resident wrapping, `fiber.rs` Service downcasts.

Consequence: deleting `memory.rs` as a whole module deletes hot reload and the in-flight-call guard. Any trim that keeps hot reload must split the struct, not drop it.
