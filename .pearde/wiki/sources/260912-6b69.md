# separating the zirkle reload transaction from the memory bank

The zirkle core `memory.rs` module fuses two mechanisms. They separate cleanly along one line: the bank keeps state, the reload transaction keeps none.

- **Bank (removable):** `Snapshot {revision, version, bank, state}`, `Inner {snapshot, frozen}`, `read()`, `update()` compare-and-swap, `fork()` (freeze-and-copy the snapshot), `thaw()`. Reached from `sdk.rs::memory`/`checkpoint`, the `{"memory":"read"|"update"}` RPC in `cartridge.rs`, `ctx:memory`/`ctx:checkpoint` in `context.rs`, `Ctx::memory` in `runtime.rs`.
- **Reload transaction (must stay):** `gate: Arc<RwLock<()>>`, `epoch: watch::Sender<u64>`, `pending: AtomicBool`, `begin()/pending()/finish()`. Reached from `loader.rs::replace_entry` and `request_reload`, all of `service.rs`, the `service is being replaced` guard in `lua.rs::to_lua`, resident wrapping in `runtime.rs::on`/`Ctx::provide`, the `Service` downcasts in `fiber.rs`.

Once the bank goes, `fork()` degenerates to `Clone` (nothing private is left to copy) and `thaw()` becomes a no-op, so both calls in `replace_entry` disappear rather than being ported. The remaining struct is three shared handles and four methods, and it carries no `serde` derive.
